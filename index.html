Discover the Beauty of Ilocos Norte

Ilocos Norte is a destination known for its rich cultural heritage, historic landmarks, natural attractions, and delicious Ilocano cuisine. From beautiful beaches to remarkable architectural treasures, the province offers experiences for travelers who want adventure, relaxation, and cultural discovery.

📍 Location

Northern Luzon, Philippines

🎯 Known For

Beaches, Heritage, Nature & Food

✨ Perfect For

Adventure, Culture & Sightseeing

Must-Visit Places

Bangui Windmills

Iconic wind turbines set against stunning coastal mountains and azure seas.

Pagudpud

Pristine white sand beaches with crystal clear waters and lush tropical palms.

Paoay Church

UNESCO World Heritage stone church showcasing Filipino-Asian architecture.

Scenic Coastal Road

Breathtaking highway hugging cliffs with turquoise waters below.
