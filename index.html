05 — Did You Know?

Stories behind
the places.

01 — Bangui

The windmills became one of the defining images of Ilocos Norte.

The turbines stand in a distinctive line along Bangui Bay, making the coastline immediately recognizable to travelers.

02 — Paoay

Paoay Church is known for its massive earthquake-resistant design.

Its broad buttresses and distinctive architecture are closely associated with the Earthquake Baroque tradition in the Philippines.

03 — La Paz

Ilocos Norte has a landscape that can feel surprisingly desert-like.

The La Paz Sand Dunes create a striking contrast with the province's beaches, mountains and historic towns.

04 — Food

Ilocano cuisine is known for bold flavors and resourceful cooking.

Bagnet, empanada, pinakbet and local vinegar are among the flavors that make a northern food journey memorable.

05 — Cape Bojeador

A historic lighthouse watches over the northern coast.

Cape Bojeador Lighthouse is one of the province's best-known heritage landmarks and a reminder of Ilocos Norte's long maritime history.

06 — Patapat

One road can turn into a destination of its own.

Patapat Viaduct follows the coastline between mountain slopes and the sea, creating one of the most memorable road views in the north.

09 — Travel Information

Plan Your Visit

Travel prepared, respect the places you visit, and leave room for the unexpected discoveries that make a northern journey memorable.

What to Prepare

  • Comfortable clothing suitable for walking and outdoor activities
  • Phone or camera for photographs and navigation
  • Personal essentials and any required medications
  • Drinking water, especially for outdoor excursions
  • Extra budget for meals, entrance fees and souvenirs

Travel Tips

  • Respect local culture, communities and heritage sites.
  • Keep beaches, viewpoints and tourist areas clean.
  • Follow local rules, posted safety instructions and site guidelines.
  • Support local restaurants, guides, shops and community businesses.
  • Check current road, weather and attraction conditions before traveling.

Best Time to Visit

Check the latest local weather forecast before your trip and plan outdoor activities with conditions in mind.

Getting Around

Plan transportation between attractions in advance, especially when combining destinations across different municipalities.

Travel Responsibly

Protect the landscapes you came to see and choose local businesses whenever possible.
