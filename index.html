01 — The North

A place
of contrasts.

Ilocos Norte brings together dramatic coastlines, wind-swept landscapes, historic architecture and unmistakable Ilocano flavors.

From Bangui to Pagudpud and Paoay, the province offers a journey that feels both timeless and alive.

02 — Destinations

Go beyond
the postcard.

Real places. Dramatic landscapes. A northern experience shaped by the sea, wind, stone and generations of Ilocano culture.

Bangui · Coast

Bangui Windmills

Saud Beach

Clear northern waters, quiet shores and wide-open skies.

Paoay Church

An extraordinary expression of Philippine Earthquake Baroque architecture.

A spectacular coastal road where mountain, ocean and sky meet.
